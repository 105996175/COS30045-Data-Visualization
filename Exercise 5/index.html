Appliance Energy Consumption

Welcome to the Appliance Energy Consumption website. This website provides information about household appliance energy consumption in the Australian market.

Understanding how much electricity appliances use can help households make better decisions about energy use and reduce electricity costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Appliance energy consumption refers to the amount of electrical energy used by an appliance while it is operating.

Energy-efficient appliances can use less electricity, which may help reduce household energy consumption and costs.

You can compare appliances by looking at their power usage, energy rating and estimated electricity consumption.
